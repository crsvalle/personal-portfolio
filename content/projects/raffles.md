Raffles is a fun and interactive app that allows users to participate in lottery-style raffles by purchasing tickets. The app enables users to view active raffles, purchase tickets, and track raffle draws. Winners are randomly selected from ticket holders, and notifications are sent to alert the winners. It's a simple and engaging way to take part in raffles on the go.
